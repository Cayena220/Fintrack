Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the FinTrack app (“FinTrack”, “we”) collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and the display name you choose.
  • Financial records you enter — accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals and recurring rules. FinTrack does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank or card credentials.
  • Technical information — the app version and platform, used to tell you about updates.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and sync your data between your devices.
  • To calculate balances, budgets, reports and reminders shown in the app.
  • To send account emails such as confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your financial records with third parties for marketing.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase, in an encrypted database. Row-level security rules ensure each record can be read or changed only by the account that created it.

So the app works without an internet connection, it also keeps a copy of your records on your device, along with any changes not yet uploaded. The copy is removed when you sign out. Changes not yet uploaded stay on the device until you sign in again and they sync.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any record in the app at any time.
  • You can export your transactions as CSV from the Reports screen.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us. Deleting your account removes all of your records permanently.

Children

FinTrack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will update this page if the policy changes and revise the date above.
