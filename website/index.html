    Frequently asked questions

    Is FinTrack free?expand_more

    Yes. FinTrack is free to download and use.

    Why isn't it on the Play Store?expand_more

    We distribute the app directly from this site. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time — that's expected. The app checks for updates automatically.

    Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?expand_more

    New installers without a paid code-signing certificate trigger SmartScreen. Click More info → Run anyway to continue.

    Is my financial data safe?expand_more

    Your data is stored in a secure cloud database with row-level security, so only your signed-in account can read or change it. We never sell your data. See the privacy policy.

    Can I use it on my phone and PC at the same time?expand_more

    Yes. Sign in with the same email on both and your transactions, budgets and goals stay in sync.

    Can I export my data?expand_more

    Yes — the Reports screen exports your transactions to a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.
